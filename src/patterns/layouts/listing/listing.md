---
title: Listing
---

The layout of a folder's `index.md`: its title and text in the article, then the folder's pages as a list
of links (the `list` view, `components/list-card`).

Formerly `layouts/_default/list.html`. Each entry there read `0001-01-01 | <title>`: no page has a date,
and Hugo printed the zero date. The card shows the title only.
